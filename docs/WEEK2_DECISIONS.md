# Week 2 Engineering Decisions: Chunking, Embeddings & Reranking

## Decision 1: Runbook Chunking Strategy (Section-Level with Metadata Header)
- **Experiment:** Evaluated `per_step` vs. `per_section` chunking, both with and without prepended metadata headers (`[DOC_TYPE | SERVICE | ENV | FAULT]`).
- **Finding:** Splitting runbooks into isolated single steps without headers caused context loss because individual steps ("Step 2: Drain connection pool") lack the microservice name and owner role. Keeping procedural runbook sections intact and prepending a structured metadata header raised `Recall@3`/`Recall@5` from 66.7% (per-step, no header) to 83.3% (per-section + header). `Recall@1` was 66.7% for both `per_step_no_header` and `per_section_with_header` (the best R@1 in this study), and `per_step_no_header` had the highest `Recall@10` at 83.3%. See `results/week2_retrieval_recall.csv`.

## Decision 2: Embedding Model & Hybrid Fusion (`bge-small-en-v1.5` + BM25 RRF k=60)
- **Experiment:** Compared lexical `BM25Okapi`, dense 384-dim embeddings (`bge-small-en-v1.5` via university endpoint Port 1035), and Hybrid Reciprocal Rank Fusion (`k=60`) across our 581-document SRE corpus.
- **Finding:** Dense embeddings capture semantic symptom descriptions, while BM25 excels on exact OpenTelemetry feature flag names (`paymentServiceFailure`) and port numbers (`5432`). On the 28 retrieval queries in this run, BM25 led `Recall@1` at 100.0%; hybrid RRF reached 57.1% `Recall@1` but 100.0% at `Recall@3`/`@5`/`@10`. Dense reached 39.3% `Recall@1` (100.0% at `Recall@3` and above only when the service filter was applied). RRF therefore did not improve `Recall@1` over BM25 on this paraphrased query set.

## Decision 3: Metadata Pre-Filtering + Reranking (`ms-marco-MiniLM` Feature Reranker)
- **Experiment:** Evaluated retrieval with and without metadata `service_filter` (derived from `get_service_deps`) followed by second-stage reranking, plus a 30-document poisoned/distractor stress test.
- **Finding:** Because our 581-document corpus contains 300 runbooks across 15 services and 2 environments (`prod` and `staging`), unfiltered search can rank a sibling service's runbook with a similar failure mode at Rank 1. In the current results, applying metadata `service_filter` before reranking did not change `Recall@1` for `hybrid_rerank` (92.9% with and without the filter; 100.0% at `Recall@3`/`@5`/`@10`). Reranking improved hybrid `Recall@1` from 57.1% to 92.9%, but stayed below BM25's 100.0% `Recall@1`. Documents flagged as poisoned or matching injection patterns are skipped by the retrieval guard.
